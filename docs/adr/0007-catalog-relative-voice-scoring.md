# ADR 0007 — Catalog-Relative Voice Scoring Deferred Until >=3 Distinct Authors

**Status:** proposed  
**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Enforced by:** Deferred until catalog has >=3 distinct authors in `authors/`

## Context

Same-house-style peer authors overlap on profile-relative scoring (74-91/100).

## Decision

Defer discriminative catalog-relative scoring until at least three distinct authors exist in `authors/`.

## Consequences

Document single-author limitation clearly in v1 while preserving clean extension point.
